This manual is for the people who make and look after a website with
q.e.d.: no programming needed, and no need to know how websites work
inside. The same pages are in the app: the :icon-life-buoy: **Manual**
button at the top right opens them in a window of their own, so you can
read them offline, next to what you're doing.

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): install q.e.d., make or open a
  site, find your way around, look at the result.
- [Pages and blocks](pages-and-blocks.md): write pages and posts, build
  them from blocks, columns and sections, link them.
- [Pictures and files](pictures-and-files.md): add photos and logos,
  files to download, and files the website needs as they are.
- [Versions](versions.md): every save is kept; go back to an earlier
  state of a page or of the whole site.
- [Backups](backups.md): keep a copy of the site on another computer,
  and work on it from two computers.
- [Publishing](publishing.md): put the website on your webspace.

Screenshots in this manual show the app with its *catppuccin-mocha*
theme (App → Theme of the q.e.d. app), so they look the same throughout.
